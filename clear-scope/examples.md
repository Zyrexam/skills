# Examples

## Good: twitter clone scoped

User: "build clone of twitter"
Bad agent: lists 20 features (DMs, spaces, ads, analytics...).
Good agent:

1. HMW: "How Might We let college friends share short updates fast?"
2. Asks: who (college group, not world), core action (post+read feed), constraint (2 weeks, solo).
3. Variations: simpler (anonymous board, no login), audience shift (classroom Q&A), 10x (100k users, fan-out needed).
4. Picks: simpler feed. Core loop: post -> follow -> feed.
5. MVP: auth, post 280 chars, follow, feed, like. Later: DMs, media. Not doing: ads (no users yet), spaces (cost), analytics (no need).
6. Risk: "friends will post daily" -> test: 5 friends in WhatsApp group for 3 days.

## Bad patterns

- "Twitter but better" with no who. Push back: who switches and why?
- MVP with 15 items. Force cut to 5.
- No Not doing list. Refuse to ship scope without it.
